# RogueForge Act 1 Plan: Frontier Expansion

## 1. Purpose

This document defines Act 1 as the phase where RogueForge stops being primarily a tutorial and becomes a repeatable frontier game.

Act 1 should teach the player how to create footholds in the world and use those footholds to support deeper expeditions.

Core loop Act 1 reinforces:

1. Prepare in Ironhaven
2. Travel to frontier or outpost
3. Explore, fight, gather, and pursue quests
4. Decide whether to push deeper or extract
5. Bank resources
6. Upgrade town, gear, robots, bases, and world state
7. Unlock new zones, systems, and opportunities
8. Repeat

## 2. Act Identity

### Act

- Act 1

### Name

- Frontier Expansion

### Function

- first real frontier phase
- first repeated expedition phase
- first foothold-building phase

### Goal

- player builds footholds in the world

## 3. New Mechanics Introduced

Act 1 should introduce:

- claim outposts
- banking at outposts
- frontier exploration
- base structures
- defender bots
- raids begin
- settlement upgrades
- first dungeon

These systems should not all arrive at exactly the same minute, but they should all belong to the Act 1 experience.

## 4. Player Loop

Act 1 player rhythm should be:

- go out
- claim outpost
- build storage
- bank resources
- push further
- return
- upgrade town
- repeat

This is the first phase where the player should feel that the world is changing because of repeated successful expeditions.

## 5. Boss Milestone

After the first major boss, the player should feel a clear settlement response:

- Ironhaven expands
- new buildings unlock
- new NPCs arrive

The important part is not only the boss reward. It is the visible proof that frontier success changes the settlement.

## 6. Current Build Mapping

The current build already supports much of this phase.

### Already Present

- expansive Verdant Fields frontier
- claimable outpost sites
- build mode
- storage structures that bank haul
- defender assignment and live defender NPC behavior
- raid pressure and raid waves
- settlement upgrades such as:
  - `workshop_tools`
  - `watchtower_network`
  - `survey_drones`
- Shadow Caves as the first clear dungeon-like progression step after the opening frontier push

### Current Best-Fit Content

The existing content that most closely maps to Act 1 is:

- the later part of `first_steps`
- `shard_hunt`
- the first claim/build/bank loop in Verdant Fields
- the first stretch of settlement upgrades

## 7. Current Gaps

Act 1 systems exist, but the phase is not yet as clearly structured as it should be.

Main gaps:

- outpost claiming, storage building, and local banking are implemented but not yet taught as a deliberate frontier progression arc
- the first dungeon is present in structure, but not yet framed as the natural extension of the foothold loop
- the first boss milestone does not yet feel like a clean Act 1 settlement transformation moment
- defender bots and raids exist, but they still read more like system features than the defining pressure of a frontier phase
- settlement upgrades are present, but their connection to “frontier success creates town growth” could still be stronger

## 8. Recommended Act 1 Structure

The clean Act 1 flow should be:

### Step 1: First Real Frontier Runs

- move beyond the safe edge of Verdant Fields
- gather meaningful field resources
- understand that repeated trips matter more than one single excursion

### Step 2: First Outpost Claim

- discover a claimable site
- reserve it
- understand that footholds change how future runs work

### Step 3: First Storage Build and Local Banking

- place a storage structure
- use it to bank haul away from Ironhaven
- learn that outposts extend safe logistics but do not remove danger

### Step 4: First Defensive Infrastructure

- place a sentry or equivalent defense structure
- assign a reserve bot
- understand that bases can be defended rather than only built

### Step 5: First Dungeon Push

- enter the first dungeon as a deeper frontier commitment
- link dungeon success to town and route progression

### Step 6: First Settlement Response

- return from a major victory
- unlock visible Ironhaven growth
- gain new services, NPCs, or facilities

### Step 7: Repeat at Larger Scale

- use new footholds, services, and routes to expand outward again

## 9. Success Criteria

Act 1 is successful when the player clearly understands:

- the frontier can be claimed and shaped
- outposts are worth building because they change expedition flow
- local banking matters
- storage and defense structures create real strategic advantages
- dungeons are the deeper extension of frontier progression
- Ironhaven visibly grows because the player wins in the field

If those lessons land, the player is ready for the stronger upgrade and specialization phases that follow.

## 10. Immediate Recommendation

Do not treat Act 1 as only “more areas after the tutorial.”

Treat it as the first phase where the player learns:

- I can build forward positions
- I can make the world safer locally
- my victories change both the frontier and Ironhaven

The next practical cleanup after this definition is:

1. make first outpost claim and first storage bank more explicitly taught
2. make the first dungeon feel like the natural escalation of frontier footholds
3. make the first major boss milestone produce a stronger visible Ironhaven expansion beat
